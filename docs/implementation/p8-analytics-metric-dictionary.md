# EPAMS P8 — Business Analytics Metric Dictionary

**Timezone Standard:** `Asia/Kolkata` (injected `Clock` bean in `AppConfig.java`)  
**Source Tables:** `service_ticket`, `service_catalog`, `approval_task`  
**Authorization Scope:** Organization-wide (`ADMIN`, `AUDITOR`); Own Department (`MANAGER`, `HR_MANAGER`, `DEPT_HEAD`, `RESOLVER`, `IT_SUPPORT`, `HR_SPECIALIST`, `FACILITIES_CREW`); Blocked (`EMPLOYEE`).

---

## Authoritative Metric Definitions

| Metric Name | Business Meaning | Exact Formula | Source Columns | Inclusion / Exclusion Rules | Known Limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Submitted Tickets (Period)** | Total service tickets created in `[startDate 00:00:00, endDate 23:59:59.999]` | `COUNT(t.id)` where `t.created_at BETWEEN start AND end` | `service_ticket.created_at` | Includes all statuses created in period. Respects `department` & `priority` filters. | Uses creation timestamp; does not reflect historical status transitions. |
| **Open Backlog (Snapshot)** | Live count of tickets currently awaiting fulfillment or approval | `COUNT(t.id)` where `t.status IN (DRAFT, SUBMITTED, PENDING_APPROVAL, PENDING_SECONDARY_APPROVAL, ASSIGNED, IN_PROGRESS, ON_HOLD)` | `service_ticket.status` | Current point-in-time snapshot (independent of date window). Excludes terminal states (`RESOLVED`, `CLOSED`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`). Reopened tickets appear once as `IN_PROGRESS`. | Historical open-backlog snapshots at past dates are not fabricated because no status-history snapshot table exists. |
| **Resolved / Closed (Period)** | Tickets created in period whose current status is `RESOLVED` or `CLOSED` | `COUNT(t.id)` where `t.status IN (RESOLVED, CLOSED)` in period | `service_ticket.status`, `created_at` | Excludes reopened tickets currently back in `IN_PROGRESS` (prevents double-counting). | Trend chart `resolvedCount` buckets by `t.resolved_at` date directly. |
| **Overdue Open (Snapshot)** | Live open tickets with an active SLA clock past `sla_target_date` | `COUNT(t.id)` where `t.status IN (SUBMITTED, PENDING_APPROVAL, PENDING_SECONDARY_APPROVAL, ASSIGNED, IN_PROGRESS)` AND `t.sla_target_date < NOW(Asia/Kolkata)` | `service_ticket.status`, `sla_target_date` | Strictly **excludes `ON_HOLD`** (paused SLA clock), `DRAFT`, and terminal states. | Uses server-computed `sla_target_date` adjusted for business hours. |
| **SLA Compliance Rate (%)** | Percentage of evaluated tickets meeting their SLA target | `ROUND((metSlaCount * 100.0) / evaluatedTickets, 2)` if `evaluatedTickets > 0`, else `null` | `status`, `resolved_at`, `sla_target_date` | **Excludes `CANCELLED` and `REJECTED`**. `RESOLVED`/`CLOSED` met if `resolved_at <= sla_target_date`; active overdue open tickets count as evaluated & breached. Returns `null` (UI displays `N/A`) when `evaluatedTickets == 0`. | Reflects final resolution timestamp on reopened tickets rather than intermediate pre-reopen attempts. |
| **Average Resolution Time (Hours)** | Mean net active hours from creation to resolution | `AVG(MAX(0, MINUTES(created_at, resolved_at) - total_paused_minutes)) / 60.0` | `created_at`, `resolved_at`, `total_paused_minutes` | Evaluated on `RESOLVED`/`CLOSED` tickets with non-null `resolved_at`. Subtracts `ON_HOLD` paused minutes. Returns `null` if 0 resolved tickets. | Wall-clock minus `ON_HOLD` pause minutes; does not subtract overnight/weekend non-business hours from raw elapsed delta. |
| **Daily Ticket Trend** | Daily creation vs resolution throughput | `submittedCount` grouped by `DATE(created_at)`; `resolvedCount` grouped by `DATE(resolved_at)` | `created_at`, `resolved_at` | Zero-filled for every calendar date in `[startDate, endDate]`. | Maximum span capped at 366 days to protect query latency. |
| **Department Workload & Backlog** | Operational load comparison per department | Grouped per `t.department` combining period volume, live open backlog, overdue open, and SLA % | `department`, `status`, `sla_target_date`, `resolved_at` | Sorted by highest `openBacklogSnapshot` DESC, then `totalSubmittedInPeriod` DESC. | Non-admin users see only their single authorized department row. |
| **Service Popularity** | Most frequently requested catalog services | `COUNT(t.id)` joined with `service_catalog` grouped by `catalog_item_id`, ordered DESC | `service_ticket.catalog_item_id`, `service_catalog.*` | Bounded to `1..25` items (`limit` param). Supports optional `category` filter. | Active/inactive catalog items with historical tickets in period are included for accuracy. |
| **Approval Pipeline Overview** | Pending, overdue, and decided approval workload | `pendingSnapshot` (`status=PENDING` split by Stage 1 & Stage 2); `overduePendingSnapshot` (`status=PENDING AND created_at < NOW - 24h`); `approvedInPeriod` / `rejectedInPeriod` by `created_at` in period | `approval_task.status`, `approval_stage`, `created_at` | Distinct from ticket counts (a two-stage ticket generates 2 approval tasks). | Uses 24-hour calendar policy threshold for pending approval overdue flagging. |
