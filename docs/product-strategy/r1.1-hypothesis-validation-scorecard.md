## 1. Detailed Hypothesis Validation Matrix (`H1`–`H4`)

### `H1` — Target Segment & Self-Hosted Sovereignty Demand
- **Hypothesis Statement:** Privacy-sensitive `100`–`1,000`-employee organisations (initial sweet spot `150`–`750`, including JSM Data Center migrants and regulated firms) experience real constraints against public multi-tenant SaaS and actively value a lightweight, single-binary self-hosted ESM platform.
- **Target Segment:** `100`–`1,000` employees (stratified across `100–149`, `150–300`, `301–750`, `751–1,000`), IT Leaders, SysAdmins, InfoSec, Ops Leaders.
- **Evidence Needed:** Concrete policy/regulatory/cost reasons why internal employee service or HR case data cannot use public SaaS, verified operational preference for simpler self-hosted footprints (vs. multi-node clusters), and actual JSM DC or incumbent self-hosted evaluation behavior.
- **Mapped Interview Questions (`r1.1-interview-execution-guide.md`):** `B6`, `B7`, `B8`, `C10`.
- **Mapped Survey Questions (`r1-customer-survey.md`):** `Q1`, `Q2`, `Q9`, `Q10`, `Q11`.
- **Supporting Evidence Collected:** *None yet (`0` entries).*
- **Contradicting Evidence Collected:** *None yet (`0` entries).*
- **Acceptance Threshold *(Internal Research Decision Criterion)*:** `>= 40%` of interviewed `150`–`750` target-segment IT/InfoSec/Ops participants (`>= 5` distinct organisations) cite verified self-hosting or data-residency requirements AND identify low-maintenance ops as a meaningful selection advantage (corroborated by survey Q9/Q11).
- **Falsification Threshold *(Internal Research Decision Criterion)*:** `< 20%` of target participants express real self-hosting need, OR `>= 70%` state they are moving strictly to cloud SaaS regardless of Data Center EOL. *(Trigger: Re-evaluate Direction C self-hosted-first positioning.)*
- **Current Status:** **`UNVALIDATED (NOT YET TESTED)`**

### `H2` — Recurring Operational Pain Intensity (Workflows, Onboarding/Offboarding, Repetitive FAQs)
- **Hypothesis Statement:** Cross-department approval/routing rigidity (`PROB-01`), fragmented onboarding/offboarding handoffs (`PROB-02`), and repetitive employee requests lacking contextual self-service deflection (`PROB-03`) are frequent, high-severity recurring pains in `100`–`1,000`-employee organisations.
- **Target Segment:** HR Ops, IT Support Leads, Department Managers, Employees across `100`–`1,000` employees.
- **Evidence Needed:** Specific incidents within the last `30–90` days (missed Day-1 setup, late access revocation, `> 48h` stalled approvals, repetitive FAQ volume, manual spreadsheet workarounds).
- **Mapped Interview Questions:** `A2`, `A3`, `A4`, `B5`.
- **Mapped Survey Questions:** `Q5`, `Q6`, `Q7`, `Q8`.
- **Supporting Evidence Collected:** *None yet (`0` entries).*
- **Contradicting Evidence Collected:** *None yet (`0` entries).*
- **Acceptance Threshold *(Internal Research Decision Criterion)*:** `>= 60%` of interviewed HR/IT/Manager participants (`>= 8` distinct organisations) report concrete, recent (`<= 90` days) operational failures in at least `2` of the `3` pain areas AND currently maintain manual workarounds (spreadsheets, email chasing, brittle scripts).
- **Falsification Threshold *(Internal Research Decision Criterion)*:** `< 30%` report concrete recent failures, OR majority report existing tools already handle approvals, onboarding, and FAQs satisfactorily. *(Trigger: Reprioritize problem focus.)*
- **Current Status:** **`UNVALIDATED (NOT YET TESTED)`**

### `H3` — Solution Differentiation & Acceptable Non-Goals
- **Hypothesis Statement:** Buyers in `150`–`750`-employee organisations prefer a decoupled, self-hosted Employee Service & Configurable Workflow Platform (with KB deflection, OIDC SSO, dry-run workflow simulation, and tamper-evident governance) that coexists with their existing HRIS/payroll—and do NOT require EPAMS to bundle full payroll/HRIS or network ITAM/CMDB.
- **Target Segment:** IT Leaders, SysAdmins, HR Ops, Support Leads, InfoSec.
- **Evidence Needed:** Neutral concept reactions (`r1.1-solution-concept-testing.md`), unprompted must-have feature lists, dealbreaker analysis (e.g., whether lack of ITAM/CMDB or core HRIS blocks adoption), and comparison against incumbents (ManageEngine, GLPI, Zammad, Frappe, Freshservice, JSM).
- **Mapped Interview Questions:** `C9`, `C10`, `C11`, `D1–D3`.
- **Mapped Survey Questions:** `Q4`, `Q6`, `Q11`, `Q14`.
- **Supporting Evidence Collected:** *None yet (`0` entries).*
- **Contradicting Evidence Collected:** *None yet (`0` entries).*
- **Acceptance Threshold *(Internal Research Decision Criterion)*:** `>= 50%` of interviewed technical buyers & HR co-sponsors (`>= 6` distinct organisations) confirm they already have HRIS/payroll and want a decoupled service/workflow layer, rate at least `2` of the `3` provisional initiatives as high-priority, AND do not treat absent CMDB/payroll as a hard blocker.
- **Falsification Threshold *(Internal Research Decision Criterion)*:** `>= 60%` declare built-in ITAM/CMDB or native payroll/HRIS a hard non-negotiable blocker, OR state free GLPI/Zammad/SharePoint already meets their needs without switching. *(Trigger: Re-scope R2 capabilities or reconsider non-goals `R1-DEC-005`.)*
- **Current Status:** **`UNVALIDATED (NOT YET TESTED)`**

### `H4` — Buying Authority, Switching Willingness & Commercial Viability
- **Hypothesis Statement:** Target organisations (`150`–`750` employees) have clear buying authority (IT + Ops/Finance with HR/InfoSec co-sponsorship), active budget currently spent on unsatisfactory internal service/workflow tools, and willingness to evaluate a self-hosted commercial/open-core alternative under predictable licensing.
- **Target Segment:** Head of IT, VP Operations, COO/CFO, Procurement/SysAdmins.
- **Evidence Needed:** Real historical purchase walkthroughs (`\$5k–\$50k` range), current annual tool spend, concrete procurement gates (SSO, support SLA, security questionnaire), and tangible next-step commitment (e.g., agreement to review R2 concept/pilot once built).
- **Mapped Interview Questions:** `C11`, `C12`, `D3` + `r1.1-commercial-validation-framework.md`.
- **Mapped Survey Questions:** `Q11`, `Q12`, `Q13`.
- **Supporting Evidence Collected:** *None yet (`0` entries).*
- **Contradicting Evidence Collected:** *None yet (`0` entries).*
- **Acceptance Threshold *(Internal Research Decision Criterion)*:** `>= 5` qualified organisations confirm active annual spend (or budgeted replacement window within `12` months), clarify procurement requirements EPAMS can realistically meet, AND `>= 3` express concrete willingness to participate in a structured design-partner review/pilot.
- **Falsification Threshold *(Internal Research Decision Criterion)*:** Buyers universally expect `\$0` free open-source with zero support budget, OR mandate multi-year SOC 2 Type II / FedRAMP certifications before even a self-hosted internal pilot. *(Trigger: Reassess commercial model & GTM before R2.)*
- **Current Status:** **`UNVALIDATED (NOT YET TESTED)`**
