## 1. Role of Survey Data in R1.1 (Strict Methodological Guardrail)
- The quantitative survey is strictly a **secondary triangulation tool** to measure prevalence of operational failures, hosting preferences, and mandatory procurement gates across a broader sample than `18`–`24` interviews allow.
- **Non-Negotiable Rule:** **A survey alone NEVER constitutes proof of willingness to purchase or product-market fit.** Positive survey ratings without corroborating behavioral interview evidence and real switching/budget signals do NOT satisfy R2 entry criteria.

## 2. Distribution Strategy & Bias Controls

| Channel | Target Persona Focus | Expected Mix | Bias Control / Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IT & SysAdmin Communities** (`r/sysadmin`, `r/jira`, Spiceworks, LinkedIn IT Ops groups) | IT Leaders, SysAdmins, Support Leads | Skewed toward IT / JSM / Self-Hosted | Tag channel source; cap single-subreddit share at `<= 40%` of total sample so `r/selfhosted` enthusiasts don't artificially inflate `H1`! |
| **HR Ops & People Ops Communities** (HR Ops Slack/Discord, LinkedIn People Ops) | HR Managers, People Ops Specialists | Skewed toward cloud HRIS / onboarding pain | Balances IT bias; tests whether HR co-sponsors care about self-hosting vs. workflow ease |
| **Mid-Market Ops & InfoSec Networks** (LinkedIn direct outreach, alumni/founder circles) | COO, VP Ops, InfoSec, Dept Managers | Mixed cloud & regulated industries | Captures economic buyer spend & audit realities |

### Data Quality & Cleaning Rules
1. **Exclude incomplete responses** (require completion through Q13).
2. **Segment strictly by headcount (Q1):** Separate `< 100` and `> 1,000` responses into an "Out-of-Band Comparison" tab rather than mixing them into core `100`–`1,000` (`150`–`750`) metrics.
3. **Deduplicate obvious spam / speed-runs** (`< 45 seconds` total completion time or contradictory tool/hosting entries).
4. **Zero PII in Git:** Strip optional Q15 follow-up emails immediately into a private encrypted outreach sheet before exporting anonymized CSV statistics.

## 3. Pre-Registered Cross-Tabulation Analysis Plan

Before seeing any data, commit to running these exact cross-tabs (prevents post-hoc cherry-picking):
1. **Q1 (Headcount Band) × Q9 (Hosting Preference):** Does self-hosted preference peak in `150`–`750` or shift toward cloud SaaS?
2. **Q2 (Industry) × Q9 (Hosting Preference) & Q11 (Mandatory Governance Gates):** Are regulated industries (BFSI, Healthcare, Legal, Defense/Mfg) measurably more self-hosting/audit-driven than Software/General SMBs?

5. **Q11 (Mandatory Security Blockers):** Exact rank order of OIDC/SAML SSO, PII masking, tamper-evident audit trails, self-hosted residency, and SOC 2/ISO 27001.
