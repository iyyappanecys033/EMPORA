# EPAMS 2.0 — Workstream D: Customer Discovery Interview Guide

**Project:** Employee Lifecycle and Service Management Platform (EPAMS)  
**Stage:** R1 — Product Discovery & Market Validation (Workstream D)  
**Status:** **`READY FOR EXECUTION — INTERVIEWS NOT YET CONDUCTED`**  
**Methodology:** Behavioral, non-leading discovery interviews (*The Mom Test* principles — strictly focused on concrete past incidents, existing workflows, and real costs; **never** pitch EPAMS or ask hypothetical "would you buy X?" questions).

---

## 1. Interview Rules for Facilitators
1. **Do NOT mention EPAMS or pitch features** during the first 35 minutes of a 45-minute call.
2. **Anchor on specific recent events** ("Tell me about the *last time*..."), never general opinions.
3. **Probe workarounds & real spend:** If a problem has zero workaround and zero budget/time spent trying to fix it, classify it as low-urgency.
4. **Capture verbatim quotes & artifacts** (anonymized screenshots of spreadsheets/checklists if volunteered) with explicit participant consent.

---

## 2. Core Behavioral Script (All Personas — First 10 Minutes)
1. Walk me through your role and how many employees/departments you support day to day.
2. What tools does your organization currently use when an employee needs help from IT, HR, Facilities, or Finance?
3. Think back to the **last 2 weeks**: what was the most frustrating internal employee request or handoff that stalled? What happened step by step?

---

## 3. Persona-Specific Deep-Dive Questions (25 Minutes)

### Track A — HR Professional (Onboarding, Offboarding & HR Privacy)
1. Walk me through the **last employee you onboarded** from offer acceptance to Day 1. Which departments were involved, and how did you track who completed what?
2. Tell me about the **last time an onboarding or offboarding step was missed** (e.g., laptop late, payroll delayed, or access not revoked on exit). How did you find out, and what was the impact?
3. What were the **top 5 questions employees asked HR last week**? Where do those answers currently live?
4. How do you handle confidential HR requests (salary disputes, medical leave, grievances) today so IT or non-HR staff cannot see them?
5. Have you ever evaluated a dedicated HR case management tool (like Zoho People Enterprise, ServiceNow HRSD, or Freshservice)? What stopped you or what disappointed you?

### Track B — IT Support Lead / Specialist
1. Walk me through how incoming tickets arrived and got assigned **yesterday**. How much time did triage/reassignment take?
2. Describe the **last time a ticket breached SLA or got stuck waiting on an approval**. What caused the delay?
3. How often do employees submit duplicate requests or tickets missing required details? What do you do when that happens?
4. How do you decide which specialist takes the next high-priority ticket? Have you noticed uneven workloads?
5. When a policy or approval rule changes (e.g., new laptop budget threshold), how long does it take to update your current tool?

### Track C — Department Manager (Approver)
1. Describe the **last 3 employee requests you had to approve**. Where did they arrive (email, Slack, portal), and how did you verify policy/budget?
2. Tell me about the last time an approval sat waiting in your queue longer than intended. What reminded you?
3. When a new hire joins your team, how do you currently check whether IT, Facilities, and HR have finished everything before Day 1?

### Track D — System / IT Administrator & Security Lead (Technical Buyer)
1. Do you currently self-host any internal IT/HR/service desk software (e.g., Jira Data Center, ManageEngine, GLPI, Zammad), or are you cloud-only? **Why?**
2. If you use Atlassian Data Center or on-premises software: how is your team planning for the 2026–2029 Data Center sunset or upcoming renewals?
3. What data-residency, privacy, or audit requirements (GDPR, India DPDP, ISO 27001, SOC 2, client security questionnaires) apply to your internal employee service records?
4. Walk me through your **last security or compliance audit** involving employee access approvals or HR/IT logs. What evidence was hardest to pull together?
5. What makes a self-hosted tool painful vs. easy for your team to maintain? (Database, SSO, upgrades, backups?)

### Track E — Employee (End User)
1. Think of the **last time you needed something from IT, HR, or Facilities**. Where did you look first, and how did you submit it?
2. How did you know what stage your request was in? Did you have to follow up manually?
3. Describe the last time you searched for an internal company policy or how-to guide and couldn't find it. What did you do next?

### Track F — Operations Leader / COO (Economic Buyer)
1. How much do you roughly spend per year across all internal ticketing, HR helpdesk, workflow, and wiki tools combined?
2. Which internal operational bottleneck costs your organization the most employee time right now?
3. If you wanted to inspect how long approvals or onboarding take across departments today, how would you get that number?

---

## 4. Closing & Problem-Ranking Questions (10 Minutes)
1. Looking across everything we discussed, which **one** operational problem would you fix first if you could wave a magic wand—and why?
2. Are you actively budgeting or evaluating replacements for any of these tools in the next 6–12 months?
3. Who else in your organization (HR, IT, InfoSec, Finance) feels this pain most acutely and would be willing to speak with us for 20 minutes?
