## 1. Strict Interviewer Discipline
- **Past Behavior Only (Minutes 0–35):** Ask exclusively about concrete events from the last `30–90` days ("Walk me through the last time..."). Never ask *"Would you use a tool that..."* or *"How much would you pay for..."* in the opening half.
- **Zero Pitching:** Do NOT describe EPAMS, show EPAMS UI, or claim any proposed concept is already built.
- **Actively Hunt for Disconfirming Evidence:** Probe why they *like* their current tool, why they *prefer* cloud SaaS, or why switching would NOT be worth the hassle.

## 2. Structured 45-Minute Execution Protocol

### Block A — Context & Concrete Recent Incidents (`0:00 – 0:15`)
1. **Role & Scope:** What departments/headcount do you support, and which tools touch internal employee requests today (IT, HR, Facilities, Finance)?
2. **Last Stalled Request:** Think of the most recent employee request (last `14` days) that got delayed or bounced between teams. Walk me through what happened, who touched it, how long it waited, and what finally unstuck it.
3. **Service Discovery & Repeated Questions:** How did employees find where to ask for help last week? What repetitive questions consumed the most staff time, and where do the answers currently live?
4. **Manual Coordination & Approval Delays:** Describe the last time an approval sat unactioned or a cross-department handoff (e.g., onboarding/offboarding) dropped a task. How did you discover the failure, and what workaround is in place today?

### Block B — Workflow Configuration, Governance & Self-Hosting (`0:15 – 0:25`)
5. **Workflow Changes:** Tell me about the last time an internal policy or approval rule changed (e.g., new spend threshold, security sign-off, offboarding step). How did you implement that change in your current tools, how long did it take, and who had to be involved?
6. **Governance & Audit Reality:** Walk me through your most recent internal or external security/compliance audit (ISO 27001, SOC 2, client audit, internal IT/HR review). What evidence regarding employee approvals, access changes, or PII visibility was hardest to produce?
7. **Self-Hosting vs. Cloud Reality:** For internal employee service and HR case records, does your organisation require self-hosting (on-prem/private VPC), prefer cloud SaaS, or mix both? **What specific policy, cost, or technical factor drove that decision?**
8. **Maintenance Tolerance:** For tools you currently self-host, how many engineering/admin hours per month is your team willing to spend on upgrades, backups, SSL, and database upkeep? What infrastructure dependencies (e.g., Elasticsearch, Redis, Kubernetes) feel too heavy?

### Block C — Competitor Switching & Jira Service Management Data Center Investigation (`0:25 – 0:35`)
*(Explicitly test switching reality — NEVER assume Atlassian Data Center EOL automatically produces demand for EPAMS.)*
9. **Incumbent Dissatisfaction:** What concrete frustrations (cost increases, paywalled modules, workflow limits, UI friction, support) have pushed your team to even *think* about replacing your current internal service tool—if any?
10. **Atlassian JSM Data Center Specifics *(Ask if applicable)*:**
    - How is your team approaching the 2026–2029 Jira Data Center sunset?
    - What specific objections prevent you from simply moving to Atlassian Cloud?
    - Which alternatives have you already shortlisted or tested (e.g., ManageEngine SDP On-Prem, GLPI, Zammad, InvGate, Frappe, Freshservice, OpenProject)?
    - What was **missing or unacceptable** in each alternative you looked at?
11. **Migration & Portability Barriers:** If you switched platforms tomorrow, what data MUST migrate (historical tickets, attachments, audit trails, user/group mappings, custom workflows)? What would make migration a non-starter?
12. **Buying, Procurement & Support Expectations:**
    - Walk me through the **last software tool** (`\$5k–\$50k/yr` range) your team actually purchased. Who initiated evaluation, who ran security/legal review, and who signed the PO?
    - What minimum vendor support SLA, documentation, SSO (OIDC/SAML), or security attestations are hard procurement gates before a new tool can be piloted?

### Block D — Neutral Concept Feedback (`0:35 – 0:45`)
- Present up to `2`–`3` neutral concept cards from [r1.1-solution-concept-testing.md](file:///c:/Users/Iyyappan%20E/Folders/SDE/Round%203-HR%20Interview/PROJECTS/Projects/EPAMS/docs/product-strategy/r1.1-solution-concept-testing.md) *(clearly stating these are unbuilt concepts under evaluation)*.
- Ask strictly:
  1. *"Which of these 3 problems—if any—matches an actual headache you experienced in the last 30 days?"*
  2. *"What is missing from this concept that would make it unusable in your organisation?"* (Listen carefully for dealbreakers: e.g., CMDB/ITAM, Slack/Teams native approvals, SAML/SCIM, attachments, payroll!)
  3. *"Would you ever evaluate an early self-hosted prototype if it solved [their #1 stated problem], or is switching cost too high right now?"*
