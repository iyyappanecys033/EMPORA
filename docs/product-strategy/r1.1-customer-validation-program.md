**Project:** Employee Lifecycle and Service Management Platform (EPAMS)  
**Stage:** R1.1 — Customer Validation Execution  
**Program Status:** **`PREPARED — ZERO INTERVIEWS / SURVEY RESPONSES COLLECTED YET`**  
**Governing Decision:** `R1-DEC-007` (`APPROVED` — Customer validation is mandatory before full R2 SRS & architecture work)

---

## 1. Program Objectives
1. **Empirically test `H1`–`H4`** (`r1.1-hypothesis-validation-scorecard.md`) using real, non-leading past-behavior evidence rather than internal assumptions.
2. **Validate or falsify the `150`–`750` employee initial sweet spot** within the broader `100`–`1,000` employee segment (`R1-DEC-002`).
3. **Test real competitor switching friction** — specifically investigating Jira Service Management Data Center migration plans, cloud objections, and incumbent satisfaction without assuming Data Center EOL automatically creates EPAMS demand.
4. **Evaluate neutral solution concepts** for Priority 1 (Knowledge-First Self-Service), Priority 2 (Configurable Cross-Department Workflows), and Priority 3 (Employee Lifecycle Orchestration) (`R1-DEC-003`).
5. **Explore buying authority, procurement gates, support expectations, and commercial models** without inventing pricing or revenue figures.

## 4. Research Rigor, Ethics & Anti-Bias Guardrails
1. **No Leading / Pitching:** Facilitators must NEVER pitch EPAMS or imply concepts are already built during discovery (`r1.1-interview-execution-guide.md`).
2. **Contradiction Logging Mandatory:** Every interview debrief must explicitly ask *"What did this participant say that contradicts `H1`–`H4`?"* and record counter-evidence alongside supporting evidence.
3. **Strict PII Hygiene:** Real names, emails, company names, and recordings are NEVER committed to the Git repository. Only anonymized participant codes (`P-001`, `ORG-001`) and size/industry tags enter `r1.1-validation-evidence-register.md`.
4. **No Premature Claims:** One enthusiastic interview ≠ product-market fit; survey responses alone ≠ willingness to purchase.
5. **Informed Consent:** Participants receive explicit verbal/written notice that this is non-sales product discovery and all insights are anonymized.
