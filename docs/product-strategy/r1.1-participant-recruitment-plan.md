## 2. Recruitment Inclusion & Screening Criteria

### Inclusion Criteria (All Required)
- Organisation size strictly between **`100` and `1,000` employees** (exclude `< 50` and `> 2,500` from core scoring; log out-of-band responses separately if encountered).
- Direct operational involvement within the past **90 days** in internal employee service requests, approvals, onboarding/offboarding, internal helpdesk administration, or IT/HR tool procurement.
- Mix of hosting models intentionally recruited: **at least 50% self-hosted or hybrid/privacy-sensitive**, and **at least 30% currently cloud-SaaS** (crucial to avoid confirmation bias by hearing *why* cloud buyers prefer SaaS!).

### Screening Questionnaire (Pre-Booking — 6 Quick Questions)
1. Approximate employee headcount (`100–149` / `150–300` / `301–750` / `751–1,000` / outside range).
2. Industry & whether regulated/privacy-sensitive (BFSI, healthcare, legal, defense/manufacturing, EU GDPR / India DPDP / ISO 27001).
3. Current job title and decision role (`Evaluator` / `Recommender` / `Co-Sponsor` / `Budget Approver` / `End User`).
4. Current internal request/helpdesk tools (`Email/Slack`, `SharePoint/Power Automate`, `JSM Cloud`, `JSM Data Center`, `Freshservice`, `Zoho People`, `ManageEngine`, `GLPI/Zammad/Frappe`, `Other`).
5. Current hosting model for internal service/HR tools (`Cloud SaaS` / `Self-Hosted VPC/On-Prem` / `Hybrid`).
6. Confirm participant has handled or governed internal employee workflows within the last 90 days.

### Recruitment Channels
1. **Direct Professional Outreach (LinkedIn):** Targeted search for IT Managers, SysAdmins, HR Ops Leads, and InfoSec Leads at `150`–`750`-person firms (non-sales research message; offer anonymized benchmark summary in exchange for 30–45 min).
2. **Atlassian Data Center & Self-Hosted Communities:** `r/sysadmin`, `r/jira`, `r/selfhosted`, Spiceworks, Atlassian Community forums (clearly labeled independent research — follow community self-promotion rules).
3. **HR Ops & People Ops Peer Groups:** HR Ops Slack/Discord communities, regional SHRM/PeopleOps forums, founder/alumni referrals.
4. **Warm Network Introductions:** Direct warm referrals from engineering, HR, and IT contacts (avoid interviewing personal friends who may give polite positive answers).
