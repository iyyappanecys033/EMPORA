## 1. Purpose & Discipline Rule

This document is a **commercial research framework**, NOT a pricing sheet, financial model, or revenue forecast.

In R1, commercial packaging (`R1-DEC-008`) was explicitly **`DEFERRED`** because zero target buyers have yet been interviewed. Setting price points or projecting ARR without buyer evidence creates false precision. The sole purpose of this framework is to gather real, behavioral evidence during R1.1 interviews (`C11`, `C12`, `D3`) and survey responses (`Q11`–`Q13`) on:

1. How target organisations (`100`–`1,000` employees; initial sweet spot `150`–`750`) actually budget for, approve, and procure internal service/workflow software.
2. Which commercial models fit their procurement rules and which trigger immediate rejection.
3. What operational, security, and support obligations are required before a buyer can sign a purchase order or run an internal pilot.

## 2. Candidate Commercial Models to Evaluate

During Section C/D of buyer interviews (`Head of IT`, `VP Operations`, `SysAdmin`, `Procurement/Finance`), present these five models neutrally—without attaching arbitrary prices—to uncover procurement friction, budget alignment, and buyer preference.

| Model ID | Commercial Model | Core Structure | Research Questions to Test | Potential Upside to Verify | Potential Friction / Risk to Verify |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`COM-01`** | **Self-Hosted Annual Subscription** | Flat annual licence/entitlement for deploying EPAMS on customer infrastructure, including updates and defined support tier | Does an annual self-hosted subscription pass OpEx procurement cleanly? Do buyers prefer annual predictability over perpetual + maintenance? | Predictable renewal structure; familiar to Jira DC / ManageEngine buyers | Buyers who equate "self-hosted" with one-time perpetual licences may resist recurring subscriptions |
| **`COM-02`** | **Per-Employee Licensing** | Annual fee scaled by total active employee count (e.g., bands such as `100–250`, `251–500`, `501–1,000`) | Does per-employee pricing feel unfair when most employees only open 2–5 requests/year (lightweight requester problem)? | Aligns with HRIS/Zoho People mental model if priced modestly | High friction if buyers compare against per-agent ITSM pricing (e.g., 10 agents serving 500 employees) |
| **`COM-03`** | **Per-Organisation (Tiered Flat) Licensing** | Single predictable organisation-wide fee by size band (or per-agent/admin seat with unlimited employee requesters) | Does unlimited employee requesters with tiered org or agent bands remove the "tax on adding HR/Facilities/Legal" that blocks cross-department ESM? | Directly solves cross-department licensing friction identified in R1 competitor analysis | Requires verifying whether `150`-person and `750`-person orgs accept tiered band boundaries |
| **`COM-04`** | **Community + Enterprise Editions (Open-Core)** | Core self-hosted platform freely usable; paid Enterprise edition unlocks governance/scale capabilities (e.g., OIDC SSO, cryptographic audit exports, workflow versioning/simulation, SLA support) | Would IT/SysAdmins adopt a free Community edition to prove internal value, and would InfoSec/Ops pay to upgrade for SSO/governance/SLA? | Lowers initial trust barrier for a newer vendor; enables bottom-up technical validation | Risk of "forever free" usage where `150`–`750` orgs never convert, or resentment if essential security (SSO) is paywalled (`SSO Tax` backlash) |
| **`COM-05`** | **Support & Implementation Services** | Paid technical support SLAs, upgrade assistance, workflow migration, or custom integration services (standalone or paired with `COM-01`–`COM-04`) | Do target orgs expect vendor-led onboarding/migration (e.g., from JSM DC or spreadsheets), or do they insist on self-service installation? | Important for risk-averse buyers who will not deploy unsupported software in production | Services-heavy adoption limits scalability if every customer requires custom consulting |

> **Critical Research Probe (`SSO Tax` Risk):** Specifically test whether gating OIDC SSO behind a paid Enterprise tier (`COM-04`) triggers InfoSec rejection or anti-vendor sentiment among `150`–`750`-employee IT leaders.

## 3. Structured Buyer, Procurement & Adoption Investigation

Every commercial interview with a decision-maker (`Head of IT`, `VP Operations`, `SysAdmin`, `InfoSec`, `Finance/Procurement`) must systematically capture answers to the seven mandatory questions below—anchored in **their last real software purchase**, not hypotheticals.

| # | Investigation Area | Core Questions to Ask (Past-Behavior Anchored) | Evidence to Record in Register |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Who pays?** *(Budget Holder)* | *"Walk me through the last internal IT or HR operations tool you bought. Which department's budget paid for it (IT, HR, Operations, or shared)? Was it pre-budgeted in annual planning or funded mid-year?"* | Exact budget owner title, annual vs. ad-hoc cycle, and whether cross-department cost-splitting stalls purchases |
| **2** | **Who approves?** *(Sign-Off Chain)* | *"Who had to sign off before the PO was issued—VP/CIO, CFO, Procurement, Legal, or InfoSec? At what spend threshold does formal executive or procurement review kick in?"* | Approval hierarchy, spend thresholds (`UNKNOWN` until reported), and average time from evaluation to signed PO |
| **3** | **Who evaluates?** *(Technical & Business Evaluators)* | *"Who ran the proof of concept or technical evaluation? How did HR Ops, Support Leads, and InfoSec participate—did they have veto power?"* | Evaluation roles, POC duration, and non-negotiable veto criteria |
| **4** | **Procurement requirements** | *"What mandatory checklists or documents must any new internal tool pass before production deployment—security questionnaire, vendor DPA, penetration test summary, SBOM, open-source licence audit, support SLA, or source-code escrow?"* | Exact list of hard procurement gates vs. nice-to-haves (especially noting whether self-hosted deployment reduces or removes cloud vendor SOC 2 blockers) |
| **5** | **Expected support level** | *"For an internal employee service and workflow tool, what support response expectation do you require—community/docs only, next-business-day email SLA, 4-hour P1 SLA, dedicated migration help, or guaranteed security patch windows?"* | Minimum acceptable support tier, security patch cadence expectations, and escalation channels |
| **6** | **Acceptable deployment & maintenance effort** | *"How much SysAdmin effort is acceptable to deploy and maintain a self-hosted internal tool? Do you prefer Docker Compose, a single binary/JAR + existing MySQL/PostgreSQL, Kubernetes Helm, or a virtual appliance? How often are you willing to run upgrades?"* | Preferred deployment packaging, database preference (MySQL 8 vs. PostgreSQL), backup/upgrade tolerance, and max acceptable admin hours/month |
| **7** | **Currently funded alternatives** | *"What tools, licences, or external support contracts are you currently paying for to handle employee requests, approvals, knowledge base, and onboarding/offboarding? What is the approximate annual spend band and renewal timing?"* | Active incumbent tools, self-reported spend bands (only if volunteered; never fabricate), renewal cycles, and pain-to-spend mismatch |

## 5. Commercial Validation Decision Criteria (`H4`)

*(Note: All thresholds below are **Internal Research Decision Criteria** established to prevent confirmation bias—NOT external industry benchmarks.)*

- **Proceed / Validated Signal (`H4` Met):**
  - `>= 5` distinct qualified organisations (`100`–`1,000` employees, majority in `150`–`750`) confirm active budget spent on existing internal service/workflow tools (or a planned replacement window within `12` months).
  - Clear, repeatable buyer/approver pattern emerges (e.g., IT Leader owns budget with HR/InfoSec co-sign) without unresolvable procurement blockers (such as mandatory FedRAMP or multi-year enterprise vendor track record for an internal self-hosted pilot).
  - At least `1`–`2` commercial models (`COM-01`–`COM-05`) show clear procurement compatibility across interviewed buyers.
  - `>= 3` qualified organisations express concrete willingness to review an R2 specification or participate in a future structured pilot once built.
- **Pivot / Re-Evaluate Signal (`H4` Falsified):**
  - Target buyers overwhelmingly expect `\$0` free open-source with zero willingness to pay for enterprise features or support.
  - Procurement universally requires multi-year SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001 vendor certification or external 24/7 enterprise support even for an isolated self-hosted pilot.
  - Budget ownership is so fractured between IT and HR that neither department will sponsor a cross-department service/workflow tool.

## 6. Strict Guardrails

1. **Never quote unapproved EPAMS prices** during R1.1 discovery interviews. Ask what they currently pay for incumbents and what licensing model fits their procurement process; do not pitch speculative price tiers.
2. **Never treat survey responses alone as commercial validation.** Survey `Q12`–`Q13` are directional only; commercial viability (`H4`) requires direct behavioral walkthroughs of past software purchases with real budget holders.
