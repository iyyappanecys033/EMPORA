## 3. Post-Validation Decision Outcomes

Once fieldwork completes and all evidence is synthesized into an R1.1 Evidence Synthesis Report, the user and product team select **one** of four formal outcomes:

| Outcome | Trigger Condition | Required Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`GO TO R2`** | All `8` criteria (`R2-GATE-01` through `R2-GATE-08`) are **`MET`** with `VERIFIED` primary evidence in `r1.1-validation-evidence-register.md` AND no falsification threshold triggered. | Update `R1-DEC-001`, `R1-DEC-002`, `R1-DEC-003` from provisional/hypothesis to **`APPROVED`** (or refined) and authorize R2 SRS & Architecture specification. |
| **`EXTEND R1.1 (MORE VALIDATION)`** | Evidence is directionally promising, but sample coverage is incomplete (e.g., `< 18` interviews, `< 10` orgs, missing `InfoSec` or `HR Ops` co-sponsors, or unresolved commercial procurement questions). | Run targeted follow-up interviews (`+6` to `+10` sessions) strictly targeting the missing segments/questions; keep R2 locked. |
| **`PIVOT / RE-SCOPE R1 STRATEGY`** | One or more falsification thresholds in `r1.1-hypothesis-validation-scorecard.md` trigger (e.g., `< 20%` want self-hosting; `>= 60%` require native CMDB/HRIS; or buyers demand a different primary workflow wedge). | Formally revise `R1-DEC-001`–`R1-DEC-005` in `R1-DECISION-REGISTER.md` and run focused validation on the pivoted hypothesis before R2. |
| **`HOLD / DO NOT PROCEED TO R2`** | Problem severity (`H2`), differentiation (`H3`), and commercial/adoption path (`H4`) fail simultaneously with no viable alternative wedge. | Keep EPAMS v1.x in maintenance mode; avoid sunk architectural/engineering investment in an unvalidated EPAMS 2.0 scope. |

## 4. Anti-Rationalization & Governance Rules

1. **No Moving the Goalposts:** Internal acceptance and falsification thresholds in [r1.1-hypothesis-validation-scorecard.md](file:///c:/Users/Iyyappan%20E/Folders/SDE/Round%203-HR%20Interview/PROJECTS/Projects/EPAMS/docs/product-strategy/r1.1-hypothesis-validation-scorecard.md) MUST NOT be lowered after interviews begin simply to force a `GO TO R2` verdict.
2. **Surveys Cannot Bypass Interviews:** Even `100` survey responses cannot satisfy `R2-GATE-02`, `R2-GATE-03`, `R2-GATE-04`, or `R2-GATE-05` without the minimum qualified depth interviews (`>= 18` across `>= 10` orgs).
3. **Polite Praise Is Not Validation:** Verbal compliments (*"Looks clean, interesting idea"*) without recent behavioral pain (`<= 90` days), active workaround/spend evidence, or concrete pilot commitment MUST be scored `Low` confidence and cannot pass `R2-GATE-03` or `R2-GATE-05`.
4. **Separate Engineering Maintenance Exception:** Per `R1-DEC-003`, routine frontend tooling maintenance (such as Vite migration or dependency hygiene) is tracked as separate engineering maintenance—not EPAMS 2.0 feature scope—and still requires explicit user authorization before any code edits.
